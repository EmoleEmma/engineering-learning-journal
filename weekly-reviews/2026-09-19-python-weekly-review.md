## Biggest Lesson

My biggest lesson this week was that a roadmap and a structured course serve
different purposes.

The roadmap helps me understand the overall direction and the concepts I need
to develop, while the course provides structured teaching and practical
exercises for learning those concepts.

I also learned that completing a lesson does not automatically mean I have
mastered the topic. Practical exercises, revision, and being able to explain
the concept without relying on notes are important parts of the learning
process.
